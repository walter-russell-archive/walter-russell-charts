## Facsimile plates — public domain, no license claimed

The facsimile images reproduce plates from the 1926 first edition of
*The Universal One*. The 1926 edition is in the public domain in the
United States. The facsimiles are faithful copies of those public-domain
pages. Faithful reproduction of a public-domain page creates no new
copyright. We claim no copyright over the facsimile images, restored or not.

Restoration used deterministic image operations only: background flattening,
one global threshold, despeckling, and removal of ink components that touch the
page edge. No pixel was invented. The published facsimile carries the full scan
resolution and was not resampled.

The unmodified Library of Congress page scans under `source_scans/loc/full/`
are published here as well. They are public-domain pages of the 1926 edition,
and no copyright is claimed over them either.

Every facsimile derives from the Library of Congress digitization of the 1926
edition. The Library of Congress states of this collection:

# R11 — Transcription log: TABLE OF THE TEN OCTAVES (Russell 1926, pp. 92–100)

Prepared 2026-09-11 (project item R11). This log records how the ten-octave element table was read from two scans,
cell by cell, and how every difference between the two readings was settled. It supports
`data/russell_1926_elements.json` and `data/russell_1926_elements.tsv` (regenerated by
`tools/elements_json_to_tsv.py`).

Working files stay in `data/elements_work/`, which is private staging and is not published. The USP-derived crops
under `usp/` and `usp_crops/` are internal transcription witnesses only; no pixel of the USP scan may reach a
publication asset.

All ten octaves are on the five table pages (1–3 on p.92, 4–6 on p.94, 7–8 on p.96, 9 on p.98,
10 on p.100). The page map (`data/loc_page_map.tsv`) lists no other `table`-class page; octave 10
ends with Omeganon + trailing lines on p.100 — **no continuation elsewhere**.

WITNESS INDEPENDENCE (per R5): the 1974 USP plates are photographic reproductions of the 1926
plates. The two witnesses therefore give SCAN-LEVEL independence only — independent imaging and
independent readers of one 1926 printing. That establishes what the book printed; it is NOT
edition-level independence and is never claimed as such.

## Protocol and order followed

1. **Pass 1 — LoC, read first.** Reader: vision (anthropic/claude-fable-5, first reader) on
   native-resolution block crops plus 2.5–4× zooms of every symbol column, mass/melting column,
   position column, rotated margin label, title and footnote. Record: `data/elements_work/pass1_loc.json`.
   Auxiliary machine reader: tesseract 5 (`--psm 4`), outputs kept verbatim in `data/elements_work/tess/loc_*.txt`.
2. **Pass 2 — USP, read second, blind.** Reader: an independent second reader in a separate context
   (vision anthropic/claude-fable-5 + its own tesseract runs, `data/elements_work/tess/usp_*.txt`),
   explicitly forbidden to open pass 1, the LoC images, or prior data. The two passes were never
   viewed side-by-side during first-pass reading; the second reader had no access to the first
   reader's output at any time (stronger than same-reader sequential passes).
   Record: `data/elements_work/pass2_usp.json`.
3. **Cell-by-cell diff** (Python): 959 row-cells (137 rows × 7 fields:
   position_col, name, typography, number, symbol, atomic_mass, melting_point_c), plus page
   furniture (titles, subtitles, margin labels, footnotes, trailing lines).
4. **Adjudication** of every difference at 4× native zoom in BOTH witnesses; dash-length disputes
   settled by deterministic ink-run measurement (longest horizontal dark run in same-scale crops).
5. **Chart cross-check**: wheel-plate labels recorded per row as a supplementary witness column
   (`witnesses.chart`), never primary.

## Anomalies in the printed table (two-witness agreed; flagged in JSON)

- **Octave 6 margin misprint**: top rotated label prints `ENDOTHERMAL EXHALATION`
  (every other octave prints `ENDOTHERMAL INHALATION`). Verified letter-by-letter at 3× in both scans.
- **Octave 9 column displacement**: on the exhalation side the SYMBOL/MASS/MELTING entries sit one
  row above their intended names as printed (Ta 181.5 2900 on the `Mate to 3L+` row, W 184.0 3400 on
  the `Tantalum` row; Os/Ir/Pt/Au on `Mate to 3I+`/`Osmium`/`Iridium`/`Platinum`; Hg/Tl/Pb on
  `Mate to 3E+`/`Gold`/`Mercury`; `Thallium` and `Lead` rows carry no symbol/mass). The USP scan
  shows curved leader lines connecting entries to intended rows. Transcribed STRICTLY row-wise as
  printed, with `layout_anomaly` noted on the octave.
- **Octave 10 number-magnitude drop**: after `1003E +` the numbers print `104‡` (TOMIUM — not
  1004‡), then `103E−` … `103A−`, then `+103−`, `+102−`, `+101−` (leading plus), ending `100=`
  (Omeganon). Positions mirror this (`+3−`, `+2−`, `+1−`).
- **Octave 7 vs 8–10 mid-tone lettering**: octave 7 exhalation mid-tones are `4E−`…`4A−`;
  octaves 8–9 use `3E−`…`3A−` (and octave 9 runs `3F`…`3M`).
- **4‡ typography split**: TOMION (o1), CARBON (o5), TOMIUM (o10) are full-height capitals;
  ATHENON, QUENTIN, CARBOGEN, SILICON, COBALT, RHODIUM, LUTECIUM are small capitals. All 0= names
  italic. (The expected "small caps on the ‡ rows" is thus refined, not contradicted: three of
  the ten ‡ names are full caps — both witnesses agree.)
- **Symbol periods are inconsistent as printed**: o1 `An. … At. Bn. Bt.` but `Io Vj Mv Tn`;
  o2 all eight with periods; o3 only `Gn.`; o5 only `He.`; o8 `Kr. Rb. Sr. Yt. Zr.` only;
  none in o4, o6, o7, o9, o10.
- **Masses**: asterisked `*2.92` (Luminon, footnote `*Approximate`), `*127.5` (Te), `*126.92` (I,
  footnote `* Note.—…`); trailing zeros kept as printed (16.00, 94.0, 96.0, 103.0, 112.40, 152.0,
  175.0, 184.0, 204.0, 208.0, 226.0); integer masses `59` (Ni), `139` (La).
- **Copy-specific (not artwork)**: USP p.100 bottom margin has a handwritten `$ 750 000`;
  assorted specks catalogued in `pass2_usp.json` notes.

## Chart witness (supplementary only) — agreements and variants

Wheel plates supply a third, book-internal witness for NAMES (capitals). Where a table row has a
chart box, `witnesses.chart` records its verbatim label; `UNKNOWN` means the chart prints an
UNKNOWN box at that position (all table `Mate to …` rows); empty means no box found (o9
`Mate to 3L+`, `Mate to 3E+`). Chart box order around the o9 mid-tone mates does not encode the
table's letter positions; matching is by name. Chart–table variants (chart never overrides table):

| table (pp.92–100) | chart | plate |
|---|---|---|
| Blackton (o1 102−, o10 +102−) | `BLACTON` | 0109 [89], 0117 [97] — verified at 2× zoom |
| Helenon (o3 301−) | `HELENINE` | 0109 [89] (frontispiece 0016 appears to agree with HELENINE) |
| TOMIUM (o10 104‡) | `TOMION` | 0117 [97] — chart reuses the o1 name |
| Nickel (o7 703E−) | `NICKLE` | 0113 [93] |
| Disbrossium (o9) | `DISBROSSIUM` | 0115 [95] — chart agrees with the table's non-standard spelling |

Every row in `data/russell_1926_elements.json` carries `witnesses.loc` and `witnesses.usp` — the
raw 7-field reading of that row from each pass (verbatim, pre-normalization) — plus
`witnesses.chart`, `status` (`agreed`/`resolved`; no `uncertain` remain), and a `resolution`
reason on every resolved row. Raw pass files, in that private staging directory:
`data/elements_work/pass1_loc.json`,
`data/elements_work/pass2_usp.json`; tesseract auxiliary outputs: `data/elements_work/tess/`;
adjudication crops: `data/elements_work/crops/` (l94_he_mp, l94_ne_mp, l94_n_mp, l94_cl_mp,
l96_kr_mp, l98_xe_mp, l98_hg_mp, l98_sa_sym, l92_sym_o1…o3, etc.).


## Post-audit amendment (2026-09-11)

R12 audit (verification/transcription_audit.md) found one chart-witness error: octave 5, position 502 (Beryllium) — the plates print `BERYLIUM` (single L), verified letter-by-letter at 4x in both scans. The `witnesses.chart` value was corrected from `BERYLLIUM` to `BERYLIUM`. The primary table cells were not touched; the audit found zero errors among the 959 primary cells.

## Second amendment (2026-09-11)

The amendment above was applied too widely. Both octave-5 rows carry the number 502 — Beryllium on the inhalation side (2+) and Oxygen on the exhalation side (2−) — and the `BERYLIUM` correction was written to both of them, so the Oxygen row wrongly recorded `BERYLIUM` as its chart witness. The plates do not: both blind chart passes read `OXYGEN` at that position (`data/audit_work/pass_loc_chart.json`, plate 0 label 44 and plate 4 label 14; the USP chart pass agrees). The Oxygen row's `witnesses.chart` value was restored to `OXYGEN` and its `notes` field rewritten to record the correction. The Beryllium row keeps `BERYLIUM`. `data/russell_1926_elements.tsv` was regenerated from the JSON with `python3 tools/elements_json_to_tsv.py`; the TSV had never carried the first amendment, so the two files now agree. No primary table cell was touched by either amendment.

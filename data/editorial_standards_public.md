# Editorial standards

Prepared 2026-09-11. These are the checks every published sentence of this edition must pass:
every scoreboard entry, every essay section, every caption. They are editorial policy, not legal
advice.

Scoreboard entries cite this list by number — "guardrail item 5 of `data/editorial_rules.md`".
That file is the internal editorial file the checks were first written in; it carries the legal
research behind them, it is private working staging, and it is not published. The checks
themselves are published here, with their numbering unchanged.

## The checklist

The checks are conjunctive. A sentence must pass all ten.

1. **Verdicts attach to claims, never to persons.** The grammatical subject of a verdict sentence
   is the claim: "The claim that … is unsupported." A verdict entry with a person as its subject
   fails review.
2. **No imputation of fraud, lying, or bad faith without documentary evidence.** "Wrong" is not
   "dishonest". Being mistaken, even grandly, is not deceit. This edition asserts deceit about no
   one, living or dead, without a document in hand that proves intent.
3. **Statements about a living organization are factual, verified in the present tense, and
   sourced.** Every sentence about an organization's current conduct, holdings, or claims carries
   a URL and a retrieval date.
4. **Opinion is framed as opinion, and its factual basis is disclosed.** "On the evidence in §3,
   we judge this claim unsupported" — never a bare epithet.
5. **The guardrail list is never asserted as fact**: elemental tones, frequency water-splitting,
   gravity as an electric effect, the deuterium and tritium "predictions", the 1941 "American
   Academy of Sciences" doctorate, the Tesla "1,000 years" quote, NORAD, "97 patents", and any
   implication of replication or peer review. These appear only as claims under examination, with
   the verdict machinery attached.
6. **No mockery.** No sarcasm, and no ridicule of Walter Russell, Lao Russell, or the University
   of Science and Philosophy. If a sentence would read as a sneer aloud, it is rewritten.
7. **Quotations are verbatim**, with source and page. Ellipses and brackets never change meaning.
   Rights and policy text is quoted exactly.
8. **Measured fact and inference are always distinguished.** An inference is marked
   `[INFERENCE]`.
9. **Corrections are invited and honored.** Factual errors are corrected promptly and visibly.
10. **No commercial use of anyone's name or likeness.** Russell's name, portrait, or picture
    never appears in advertising, on merchandise, or in anything that reads as an endorsement.

## Notes for reviewers

- When the choice is between "false claim" and "claim not supported by the cited evidence", write
  the second. It is more precise, and it shows its own work.
- The method carries the standard: verbatim documents, named sources, dated retrievals, and
  verdicts a reader can re-derive from the artifacts.
